## Result before correction

- Audited: 121/121 entries.
- Passed without a story-quality change: 56.
- Blocked and rewritten: 65.
- Main blocking pattern: a concrete scene was present, but the sound cue depended on an invented romanized character name such as “Hito,” “Hon,” “Naka,” “Cho,” or “Komi.” Those labels merely printed the reading in Latin letters and did not give the learner a natural retrieval hook.
- Secondary blockers: a pronunciation note replaced the required usage clarification; an English cue introduced a wrong long vowel; or the scene asserted a false exact homophone.

The exact replacement wording is the current `readingMnemonic` value for each ID below. No `readingMap`, reading, meaning, context sentence, JMdict evidence, or other field was intentionally changed during this correction pass.
