# Kakehashi: US-first App Store and Google Play growth plan

Research completed 16 September 2026. Primary market: United States; secondary: Spain. This audit used public store listings, a signed-in Google Play Console session, live Google Play web searches, the local review-request implementation, and current Apple/Google documentation. The original audit made no store or app-code changes; the subsequent authorized submission is recorded below.

## Implementation update: 16 September 2026, 09:29 UTC

The user authorized priority 1 and publication without a new build. The English-US default Google Play listing was updated and submitted with exactly two changes:

- App name: **Kakehashi for WaniKani** (22/30 characters).
- Short description: **WaniKani reviews and lessons, plus Japanese reading and listening practice.** (75/80 characters).

Publishing overview verified both entries under **Changes in review**, with automated quick checks still running. **Managed publishing is off**, so publication is automatic after successful checks and Google approval. This records an accepted submission, not a verified live public listing or a ranking improvement. No new app bundle was built or submitted; the existing production build remains 1.4.8 (38). Full description, images and other locales were not part of this submission. The research observations below are the pre-change baseline.

## Diagnosis

**Android has a concrete search-positioning gap; iOS has a plausible conversion opportunity, with the exact ranking cause still unproven.** Neither platform ranks apps by descending rating count. Apple considers searchable metadata and user behavior; Google explicitly gives query relevance substantial weight in search. Neither publishes a formula that can establish why Kakehashi occupies one exact position. [Apple search](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/), [Google discovery and ranking](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9958766?hl=en)

| Question | Verified evidence | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Why can iOS rank seventh despite more ratings? | Kakehashi has 82 US ratings at 5.0; Tsurukame has 634 at 4.7. Other checked companions have fewer ratings than Kakehashi. | The user's relative-rating observation is supported within the checked set. It does not establish relative downloads, response to search results, or ranking eligibility. Broader Japanese apps also compete for the query. |
| Is WaniKani absent from the iOS name? | No: the US name is **Kakehashi - For WaniKani**. Spain already has a Spanish name/subtitle. | Adding the same word again is not the missing iOS fix. Audit existing keywords and conversion first. |
| Is Android generally missing from Google Play? | Live production in 177 countries, including US and Spain; published target SDK 36; public listing accessible. | No evidence of a country rollout or obsolete target-SDK restriction. Device-specific eligibility still varies. |
| Is the Android issue query-specific? | In the same signed-in US/English web context, **wanikani** returned 20 apps without Kakehashi; **kakehashi** returned Kakehashi as the lead result. | Strong evidence of query-specific retrieval/prominence trouble in this web context, rather than universal catalog absence. |
| What Android metadata is actually live? | Name **Kakehashi**; short description **Wanikani Companion App**; only en-US listing supplied. | Put the service association in the name and explain the useful work in the short description. Add Spanish localization afterward. |

Public rating sources: [Kakehashi US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kakehashi-for-wanikani/id6757765444), [Tsurukame US](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tsurukame-for-wanikani/id1367114761). These observations are **not native Android search rankings**. The US parameter/footer does not prove the signed-in account has US country settings. The user's native iOS rank seven was not independently reproduced. Apple's catalog API did retrieve Kakehashi for WaniKani in both countries, which establishes catalog retrieval, not native order.

## Make these changes in priority order

### 1. Correct Android's name and short description

Recommended English title: **Kakehashi for WaniKani** — 22/30 characters.

Recommended short description: **WaniKani reviews and lessons, plus Japanese reading and listening practice.** — 75/80 characters.

Lead the full description with the review/lesson workflow and account requirement. Then explain reading, listening and progress as reasons to choose this companion. Maintain the independent-app disclaimer. The existing description already mentions WaniKani, so do not claim the app has no keyword association or prescribe arbitrary repetition percentages. Smouldering Durtles is discoverable with no WaniKani in its title, illustrating that a title change is an opportunity, not a sufficient ranking condition. [Smouldering Durtles](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.smouldering_durtles.wk), [Google listing limits](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859152?hl=en)

After capturing the current baseline, publish the focused English metadata change and record when it actually becomes live. Title changes are not part of the normal store-listing asset A/B test; treat subsequent ranking movement as an observational before/after result.

### 2. Test screenshots that immediately prove the WaniKani workflow

The actual US iPhone first three images show **Master Kanji / With Smart SRS**, **Practice / With Real Media Context**, and **Learn With Song Lyrics**. The first shows subject details; the next two emphasize immersion. None of those headlines names WaniKani, and none clearly demonstrates answering reviews or taking lessons. [Current iPhone listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kakehashi-for-wanikani/id6757765444?platform=iphone)

The Android sequence starts with subject details, a dashboard and text/voice/camera search, and uses visibly iPhone-shaped frames with Dynamic Islands. Replace these with genuine Android captures. [Current Android listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.portego00.kakehashi&hl=en&gl=US)

Test one treatment with this opening sequence:

1. **WaniKani reviews, your way** — an active review question, clearly readable.
2. **Lessons and progress in one place** — the relevant real interface.
3. **Put your kanji to work** — one distinctive NHK reading or anime-listening workflow.

The hypothesis is that a WaniKani searcher first needs confidence that their existing study routine works here, then a reason to prefer Kakehashi. This is not proof that the current assets caused low ranking. Apple can display up to three screenshots in search, making the opening sequence particularly worth testing. [Apple search presentation](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/)

### 3. Refine iOS metadata without assuming it is broken

Keep the current iOS name initially. After the screenshot experiment, test **Kanji reviews, lessons & vocab** as the subtitle (30/30 characters). Audit the private keyword field alongside it: retain useful terms, remove name/subtitle duplicates, and do not insert companion competitor names. The copy file contains a keyword candidate paired with the proposed subtitle; it is based on product relevance, not measured search volume.

App Store Connect remained signed out. Current hidden keywords, acquisition trends and conversion by storefront were therefore unavailable. Rewriting the description may help the download decision, but it is not a substitute for Apple's documented searchable fields; promotional text does not affect search ranking. [Apple keyword guidance](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/)

### 4. Improve Spain separately

iOS already has Spanish listing text. Google Play has only en-US. Add an es-ES listing with **Kakehashi para WaniKani** and the prepared Spanish descriptions/screenshots. Preserve accurate disclosure of interface language; store localization does not establish that the app interface is translated. Evaluate Spain separately so a smaller market does not obscure US results.

### 5. Improve authentic review collection and release quality

The local implementation makes one automatic native review request after a five-day streak and persists a permanent per-user marker on that device. A successful request can silently show no dialog, yet still consume that one local opportunity. It also records the request as a rating click. Recommend a conservative date-based cooldown after meaningful completed study, and separate request attempts from intentional store-link clicks. Do not measure either as a submitted review. Verify that this working-tree logic matches the shipping build before changing it. Details and sources are in the copy/review audit.

The published Android release has a genuine optimization warning: 1% DEX obfuscation, with a **February 2027** fix deadline. Plan a tested optimized release. This future requirement is **not evidence of a current search penalty**. The latest version's crash/ANR data was unavailable, so no clean-vitals conclusion is justified. The bundle also lists camera, microphone and portrait hardware features; inspect an affected device if direct installation fails. [DEX guidance](https://developer.android.com/topic/performance/vitals/code-optimization), [Google technical quality requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/17492799?hl=en)

## How to tell whether it works

The observed Play listing report for **16 August–12 September 2026** showed **237 visitors, 97 unique user install clicks and 41% CTR**. These are current listing interaction metrics, not 97 confirmed installations or a retention rate. No listing experiments were configured. This is a small traffic pool: use one treatment and the console's sample estimate, and allow inconclusive results.

Separately, Grow for **19 August–15 September** showed 105 device acquisitions and 7.37K device impressions. Do not combine the two date windows into a conversion calculation. Grow's Paid/direct bucket includes branded search, so the lack of a separate Search row is not proof of no search traffic. [Google reporting definitions](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859173?hl=en)

| Stage | Action | Decision criterion |
|---|---|---|
| Baseline | Save existing metadata, 28-day store metrics, and several comparable native searches for US and Spain. Record device, storefront/account, language, date, prior installation, and ads. | Separate eligibility, low visibility and low conversion. Record “absent from first N results,” not “unindexed.” |
| First intervention | Android title/short-description change; independently, iOS first-screenshot treatment with metadata held stable. | More qualified listing visits/downloads; better observed organic presence across repeated checks. Do not attribute all movement to one change without controls. |
| Subsequent evaluation | Review after roughly 2–4 weeks once changes are live; extend if exposure is too small. | Compare like periods/countries, annotate releases and promotion, use the platform's experiment confidence rather than a few installs. This is an evaluation window, not a promised ranking timeline. |
| Next intervention | iOS subtitle/keyword refinement; Spanish Play localization; then Android screenshot experiment after enough traffic. | Keep only improvements supported by user response, with retained/activated learners checked separately from clicks. |

Track **consistent US organic top-three presence**, **share of comparable checks at number one**, **first-time downloads**, and **learners completing their first study session**. Set numeric traffic/activation targets after the baseline is available. App Store Connect lacks keyword-level analytics, and Apple Search reporting can include ads, so do not label that source as pure organic WaniKani traffic. [Apple WWDC26 analytics discussion](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/8010/)

No credible plan can guarantee first result for every user, query context and time. Paid search buys exposure rather than guaranteed organic rank; even Apple Ads does not let advertisers choose a specific search-result slot. Do not spend on ads until the listing and activation measurement are ready. [Apple Ads placements](https://ads.apple.com/app-store/help/ad-placements/0082-search-results)
