# Kakehashi: Apple App Store ASO research

Researched 16 September 2026. Priority: United States; secondary market: Spain. This memo covers public evidence and proposed experiments, not authenticated App Store Connect analytics. No store metadata or campaigns were changed.

## What the evidence says

Having the second-most ratings among WaniKani companion apps does not imply second position for the query. Apple combines relevance of the title, subtitle, keywords and primary category with downloads, ratings, reviews and other behavior. It does not publish the weights or a formula that converts rating count into rank. Kakehashi already has the exact term WaniKani in its title. There is no obvious missing-name-keyword problem on iOS. [Apple search guidance](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/)

Kakehashi's public listing was present in both markets. Its current US title is `Kakehashi - For WaniKani` (24 characters) and subtitle is `Learn Japanese Kanji & Vocab` (28 characters). Spain displays `Kakehashi - Para WaniKani` and `Aprende Japonés Kanji y Vocab`; Spanish listing localization already exists. [US listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kakehashi-for-wanikani/id6757765444), [Spain listing](https://apps.apple.com/es/app/kakehashi-para-wanikani/id6757765444)

The public ratings are territory-specific. A worldwide total in App Store Connect must not be compared with another app's US or Spanish product-page count. Ratings without text and written reviews are also different measures. Apple explains that each country or region has its own overview rating. [Ratings overview](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/monitor-ratings-and-reviews/ratings-and-reviews-overview)

## What to change first

1. **Keep the exact WaniKani association in the name.** Retain the current title for the first test. Reordering words is not a verified shortcut: Apple's public documentation does not state a positional keyword weight. A redesign of the entire brand would add noise before the real acquisition bottleneck is known.
2. **Make the review-and-lesson use case the first message.** The screenshot audit above shows that the first three images currently emphasize subject information, anime examples and lyrics. My hypothesis is that someone searching WaniKani primarily wants to do their existing reviews and lessons; real-content features become the reason to choose Kakehashi afterward. Proposed first three screenshot messages: “WaniKani reviews, your way”; “Lessons and progress in one place”; “Put your kanji to work.” Show an active review question, the lesson/progress interface and then one real-content use case. This is a conversion hypothesis, not evidence that current screenshots caused the ranking. The [public listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kakehashi-for-wanikani/id6757765444) confirms these feature groups exist.
3. **Audit the private keyword field before replacing anything.** Keep useful existing terms and remove duplication. Under the current name/subtitle, a provisional 93-byte ASCII candidate is `reviews,lessons,srs,offline,flashcards,radicals,reading,listening,jlpt,practice,widgets,study`. Verify actual functionality and search demand before submission; this is not measured keyword-volume research. Do not add competing companion names. Apple's search guidance says to avoid repeating title/subtitle/category terms, irrelevant terms, and unauthorized protected terms. [Keyword guidance](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/search/)
4. **Test a more task-specific subtitle separately.** Candidate: `Kanji reviews, lessons & vocab` (30 characters). Move any useful displaced concepts, such as Japanese, into the private keyword field and remove newly duplicated terms. Apple allows 30 characters each for title and subtitle; name changes normally accompany a new version. [Metadata limits](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-information/)
5. **Maintain authentic rating collection.** Preserve the existing excellent summary rating. Ask after completed study tasks using the system review prompt; do not interrupt a review session. Apple's guidance permits the system prompt up to three times per 365 days and cautions that resetting ratings can weaken social proof. [Ratings guidance](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/ratings-and-reviews/)

Apple's platform reference says the keyword field allows 100 bytes, while its marketing guide says 100 characters. ASCII avoids the discrepancy; validate non-ASCII localized keyword lists in App Store Connect. Promotional text is useful conversion copy but does not affect search ranking. The description is used for web-engine results; do not assume stuffing it provides native App Store keyword weight. [Platform metadata reference](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/platform-version-information), [Product-page guidance](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page/)

## Test design and measurement

**Baseline:** Collect at least two comparable weeks of US and Spain data separately. Record device, OS version, storefront, store language, whether the app was previously downloaded, exact query, time, visible position and organic position excluding ads and non-app cards. Capture the same queries (`wanikani`, `wani kani`, `kakehashi`, plus relevant task phrases) on multiple days. A single personal search is useful evidence of that experience, not a universal rank.

In App Store Connect, compare impressions, first-time downloads, product-page views and conversion by country and App Store Search source. Keep acquisition source, app version, seasonality and paid campaigns in view. **There is no keyword-level data in App Store Connect** according to Apple's WWDC26 group lab. App Store Search analytics can blend ads with organic traffic. Apple recommends different custom pages for ads and organic search when separation matters. [WWDC26 group lab, 57:14](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/8010/)

**First experiment:** One screenshot treatment focused on reviews and lessons, with the current title/subtitle held stable. Apple Product Page Optimization supports icons, screenshots and previews, with up to three treatments and up to 90 days. Use its traffic/duration estimate; a small app may not have enough traffic for a conclusive result. Do not declare a winner after a handful of downloads. Prefer one treatment to splitting scarce traffic. [PPO setup](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/create-product-page-optimization-tests/overview-of-product-page-optimization), [PPO duration and configuration](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page-optimization/)

**Second experiment:** A subtitle/keyword release after the first experiment, judged against comparable pre/post periods. PPO is not a native title/subtitle-ranking A/B test. A sequential result can be confounded by outside changes; annotate it accordingly. Apple's newer analytics page mentions descriptions among PPO elements, while its setup documentation lists icons/screenshots/previews, so check the actual console before designing a description experiment. [Current analytics documentation](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect-analytics/acquisition/product-page-optimization)

**Alternative organic page:** Apple now lets approved custom product pages appear for selected keywords from the latest approved app version. This can give WaniKani intent a dedicated page while preserving broader positioning elsewhere. Verify which approved keywords the console makes selectable, assign the appropriate term, and measure that page's search acquisition. This is an intent-matching and measurement tool, not a guaranteed rank boost. CPPs do not use standard PPO tests. [CPP setup and keyword assignment](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/create-custom-product-pages/configure-multiple-product-page-versions)

## Can Kakehashi be first always?

No defensible organic guarantee is possible from the public evidence. The practical targets are consistent top-three organic presence in the US, an increasing share of checks at number one, and more first-time users who finish their first study session. Numeric improvement targets should be set after the baseline is known.

Apple Ads can purchase additional search exposure, but cannot guarantee first placement either. Ads can appear at the top or farther down; relevance and bids influence auction eligibility, and advertisers cannot select or bid for a particular position. [Apple Ads placements](https://ads.apple.com/app-store/help/ad-placements/0082-search-results)

If a paid trial is desired later, use a bounded US Manage Bids campaign on the exact WaniKani intent and measure cost per activated learner, not just installs. Monitor **Impression Share (First Ad Position)** separately from **All Ad Positions**; Apple now reports both. This is a proposed future experiment, with no budget spent or campaign created. [Keyword targeting](https://ads.apple.com/app-store/help/keywords/0014-add-and-manage-keywords), [Ads reporting definitions](https://ads.apple.com/app-store/help/reporting/0023-reporting-options-and-definitions)

The unresolved evidence needed for a causal diagnosis is Kakehashi's private keyword/localization configuration, country/source acquisition trend, conversion and retention, the actual native result sets with ads identified, and comparable competitor downloads. Public rating totals alone cannot supply those answers.
