Overview

Kakehashi is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your information.

Data We Collect

Kakehashi collects and stores the following data locally or, where noted, for account-backed features:

  • WaniKani API Token: Stored securely on your device. It is sent over HTTPS to WaniKani's official API and, for account-backed features such as combined study-time history, to Kakehashi's verification endpoint. Kakehashi uses it only to verify your WaniKani identity and does not store it.
  • App Settings: Your preferences such as review batch size, display options, and notification settings are stored locally on your device.
  • Cached WaniKani Data: Subject data (kanji, vocabulary, radicals) is cached locally to improve performance and enable offline access.
  • App Usage and Study-Time Records: Daily foreground-time totals may be stored by account and device to provide combined study-time history and usage streaks.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not store your WaniKani API token on Kakehashi's backend
  • We do not use third-party advertising trackers
  • We do not transmit your core learning data to Kakehashi's backend unless you use a server-backed feature
  • We do not sell your data or share it with third parties for advertising
  • We do not store your WaniKani password

Third-Party Services

Kakehashi connects to the following third-party services:

  • WaniKani API: To fetch your learning data and submit review results. Please refer to WaniKani's Privacy Policy for information about how they handle your data.
  • Kakehashi API: To verify your WaniKani identity, combine study-time history across devices, and provide other server-backed features.

Data Storage

Most data is stored locally on your device using secure storage mechanisms provided by iOS/Android. Your API token is stored using the device's secure keychain/keystore and is not retained by Kakehashi's backend after identity verification. Server-backed features store only the records needed to provide those features.

Data Deletion

You can delete locally stored app data at any time by:

  • Logging out of the app (clears your API token and cached data)
  • Uninstalling the app (removes all locally stored data)

Logging out or uninstalling does not automatically delete account-backed records already stored by Kakehashi. To request deletion of those records, contact kakehashi.app@gmail.com.

Children's Privacy

Kakehashi does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
