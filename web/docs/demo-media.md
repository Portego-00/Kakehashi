# Demo reading samples

`frieren/page-01.jpg` through `page-14.jpg` are the first 14 spine pages of the user's existing **葬送のフリーレン（１）** EPUB import, by 山田鐘人 and アベツカサ, published by 小学館. These are unmodified image bytes from the supplied local file, included at the user's request as a small test excerpt. The sample opens on page 13, whose speech balloons were checked against the image and transcribed for immediate dictionary and translation use. The original 200-page import and its reading progress are untouched.

`rainy-afternoon.txt` is an original Kakehashi demo story.

The video samples link to the original creators' YouTube videos:

- Japanese with Shun: <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K5RfTVZH-1g>
- Nihongo-Learning: <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P24Rtgi22FI>

Japanese timed captions for both URLs were verified on 2026-09-07 through the app's existing `youtube-transcript.ai` importer. The demo stores only short verified caption excerpts in code; opening a video requests its available full Japanese captions through the normal import route. Successful imports persist in the browser's isolated demo library. Video playback remains on YouTube.
